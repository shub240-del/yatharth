# V0.2 dataset and review protocol

Version: yatharth-v02-synthetic-1.0. All 260 examples and proposed labels were authored by the assistant; no private messages, scraped corpus, or real-world case collection was used. The author had inspected the rules, so this is neither blinded nor an independent held-out test. Product tuning on this challenge set: none.

The dataset contains 64 scenario groups: 58 English/Hindi/Hinglish scenario families (174 rows) and six exploratory Marathi cases. Eight variants of ten English seeds add 80 rows, for 260 total. Of these, 236 are scored (161 serious, 75 nonserious), and 24 ambiguous cases are excluded. Correlated translations/variants do not constitute independent people or observations.

## Schema and label policy

Each row carries id, group_id, language, message, expected_risk_level, expected_category, expected_signals, caregiver_escalation_expected, buckets, source_type, source, dataset_version, variant, parent_id, label_rationale, reviewer_1, reviewer_2, final_label, agreement, label_status, split, and context_available. Late-request cases also retain conversation_turns. Proposed labels are separate from final human labels.

LOW means routine, completed transaction, or safety education; MEDIUM means isolated suspicion; HIGH means actionable risk with corroborating evidence; CRITICAL means stronger coercive authority/emergency/secrecy/legal threat; AMBIGUOUS means insufficient context or disagreement. Full policy: evaluation/v02/PROTOCOL.md. Categories/signals use the frozen engine ontology for comparability; this constrains what can be annotated and is a study limitation.

## Human review — pending

Completed independent pairs: 0. Agreement and Cohen kappa: N/A. No row is human-validated. The human-only evaluation currently scores zero cases; proposals cannot substitute for ground truth.

Two shuffled JSONL packets omit model predictions and proposed labels. Reviewers independently fill reviewer_id, reviewed_at, is_human, risk_level, category, signals, escalate, and notes. The operator must verify real reviewer identity, competence, and independence; the software's attestation is not identity verification. Send each reviewer only their packet and a neutral label rubric, not this failure report or predictions. Use fluent/native reviewers for each language.

The merge validates IDs, input version/text, distinct reviewers, ontology, and severity/escalation consistency. Missing paired reviews retain no final label. Any disagreement on risk, category, signals, or escalation creates final AMBIGUOUS while retaining both annotations. Risk agreement, full-label agreement, pair coverage, and risk-level Cohen kappa are reported separately. Constant-label kappa is undefined. For partial batches, provide only completed JSONL records, not untouched blank rows.

## Limits and provenance

Translations and transformations have not been independently checked for fluency or semantic equivalence. Some adversarial edits retain conspicuous keywords. Marathi n=6 is exploratory only. Late-request snippets are concatenated into one input, not conversation-memory tests. Reserved .invalid domains and documentation IP 192.0.2.10 are inert examples and were not fetched. No population prevalence, fairness, external validity, or calibrated probability claim is justified.
