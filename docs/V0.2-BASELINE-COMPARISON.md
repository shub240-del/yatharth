# V0.2 baseline comparison — 2026-10-05

All figures use provisional assistant-authored labels. There are 236 scored cases: 161 serious and 75 nonserious; 24 ambiguous cases are excluded. No human ground truth or representative prevalence exists.

## Models and primary result

A is a deliberately simple normalized English keyword OR detector, with 14 terms; a hit produces HIGH and three hits CRITICAL. A2 adds Hindi/Hinglish/Marathi lexical equivalents as a sensitivity analysis. Both are evaluation-only. Their exact fixed vocabularies appear in benchmark.py. B is frozen Yatharth rules-0.1+decision-0.1 at thresholds 60/80. C is the existing bounded optional AI path: NOT RUN because no live provider credentials/model were configured. No fallback result is presented as AI performance.

| Model | TP | TN | FP | FN | Precision | Recall | F1 | FPR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A_keyword | 97 | 62 | 13 | 64 | 88.18% | 60.25% | 71.59% | 17.33% |
| A2_multilingual_keyword | 129 | 56 | 19 | 32 | 87.16% | 80.12% | 83.50% | 25.33% |
| B_yatharth | 65 | 73 | 2 | 96 | 97.01% | 40.37% | 57.02% | 2.67% |


Yatharth reduces false alerts from 13 to 2 versus A, but misses 96 serious examples versus 64. Its higher precision comes with lower recall and F1. It does not dominate the simple baseline. A2 detects more serious cases but raises more false alerts; this shows the importance of vocabulary coverage, not superior contextual reasoning. Baseline definitions and synthetic class mix affect all comparisons.

Precision = TP/(TP+FP), recall = TP/(TP+FN), F1 = 2TP/(2TP+FP+FN), FPR = FP/(FP+TN). Serious means a proposed HIGH or CRITICAL label. Undefined denominators remain null/N/A. Ambiguous alert rates are A 12.5%, A2 16.67%, B 0%; absence of alert is not correctness on an ambiguous case.

## Uncertainty

## Optional AI limit

The current AI gate can add at most 10 points, requires confidence >=0.8 and overlap with at least two existing signals, and cannot add signals or reduce scores. Only seven current misses can cross the alert threshold under an optimistic maximum bonus. Thus the best structural bound here is 72/161 = 44.72% recall, with existing false alerts retained. This is code analysis, not a live AI result or a claim about another AI architecture. The seven IDs are recorded in metrics.json.
